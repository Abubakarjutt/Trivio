## Problem Statement

Career switchers (especially non-CS to tech) make irreversible career and education decisions based on outdated advice from family, noisy content from YouTube, or no guidance at all. By the time they realize they needed expert mentorship, the switching cost is enormous — years of wrong education, lower-paying roles, and painful uphill transitions.

No existing platform specifically matches career switchers with mentors who have made the *exact same transition*. MentorCruise, GrowthMentor, and Clarity.fm offer general tech mentorship but don't organize around transition paths ("Mechanical Engineering -> Cloud Support -> DevOps"). The founder lived this pain: non-CS background, pivoted to IT via masters, hit a ceiling due to lack of CS foundation, and believes earlier access to the right mentor would have changed his trajectory.

## Demand Evidence

- Founder experienced this pain firsthand over multiple years
- The cost of bad advice is measurable: years of education in the wrong field, salary differential of $30-80K/year between the wrong career and the right one
- Current alternatives (family advice, YouTube, LinkedIn cold outreach) are demonstrably failing — most people don't even know they should seek professional mentorship
- Existing platforms (MentorCruise at $50-400/month) have proven people will pay for mentorship — but none specialize in career-transition matching

## Status Quo

People currently solve this problem through:
1. **Family and friends** — advice is 10-20 years outdated, based on a different job market
2. **YouTube** — generic, noisy, no accountability, no personalization
3. **LinkedIn cold outreach** — low response rate, usually attempted too late (post-education)
4. **They wing it** — most people settle for no guidance and discover mistakes years later

The critical insight: most people don't deliberately seek professional mentorship because they don't know it's an option. The pain is invisible until it's too late.

## Target User and Narrowest Wedge

**Primary user:** Career switcher, aged 23-30, currently working in a non-tech field, considering a masters degree or bootcamp to transition into tech/IT. They're about to commit $10-50K and 1-2 years to this decision. A 45-minute call with someone who made their exact transition could save them from a catastrophic wrong turn.

**Wedge:** Paid 1:1 sessions ($30-75) with mentors who have made specific career transitions. Start with non-CS-to-tech transitions. 5-10 mentors, first 20 mentees. The "exact match" is the product — not "tech mentor" but "person who switched from accounting to software engineering 3 years ago."

**Reframe (from Codex second opinion):** This is a "pre-decision risk-reduction product." The highest-value moment is *before* someone commits money and time. Build around career-decision clarity, not open-ended mentorship.

## Constraints

- Solo founder/developer
- Budget: bootstrapped (no external funding yet)
- Timeline: Week 0 supply validation + 4 weeks build = live by week 5 (buffer for integration hiccups)
- Must build trust — strangers paying strangers for career advice requires visible credentials
- Target demographic may be in developing countries (price sensitivity, mobile usage)

## Premises

1. Career switchers will pay $30-75 for a 1:1 session with someone who made their exact transition — because the ROI is measurable (salary difference)
2. Key differentiator vs MentorCruise/Clarity: "exact match" transitions, not generic "tech mentor"
3. Supply side (mentors) can be bootstrapped from LinkedIn/Reddit career-switch communities
4. **Revised:** Web-first with trust-building features (LinkedIn verification, career timeline, reviews). Mobile app comes after validating 20+ paid bookings
5. Content marketing (short-form video, SEO) solves the "people don't know they need this" discovery problem

## Cross-Model Perspective

Codex independently analyzed the session and offered these insights:

**Prototype suggestion:** Concierge marketplace — landing page, mentee intake form, mentor cards showing exact transitions, pay-to-book CTA, post-call feedback form. Skip: mobile app, ratings, chat, subscriptions, AI features, group sessions.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Concierge MVP
Landing page with mentor cards, Stripe payment links, Cal.com scheduling. Manual matching via WhatsApp/email. 1-2 weeks, low risk, but looks basic and doesn't scale past ~50 mentees.

### Approach B: Full Marketplace Platform (Web + Mobile)
Complete vision with React Native mobile app, video calls, ratings/reviews, group sessions. 4-6 months. Professional but extremely high risk — building for months before any user interaction.

### Approach C: Trust-First Web Platform (Chosen)
Proper webapp with LinkedIn OAuth career verification, mentor timeline visualization, booking + payment, post-session reviews. No mobile app yet. Ships in 3-4 weeks. Balances trust-building with speed to market.

## Recommended Approach: Trust-First Web Platform

**Why:** The founder's core concern is trust — strangers won't pay strangers without visible proof of expertise. A landing page alone doesn't build enough trust. But a 6-month full build is too risky pre-validation. Approach C gives LinkedIn-verified career timelines, real reviews, and a professional booking experience in 3-4 weeks.

### Tech Stack
- **Framework:** Next.js 15 (App Router) + TypeScript
- **DB:** PostgreSQL + Prisma ORM
- **Auth:** NextAuth.js v5 — magic link (Resend) for both mentors and mentees. Mentor verification is manual: founder checks LinkedIn profile URL and marks as verified. LinkedIn OAuth deferred to v2 (removes API approval dependency and auth complexity for 5-10 mentors)
- **UI:** Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui
- **Payments:** Stripe Payment Links (platform collects payment, pays mentors weekly via bank transfer/PayPal). Migrate to Stripe Connect in v2 when volume justifies onboarding complexity
- **Scheduling:** Cal.com embed (mentor creates a Cal.com account, embeds their booking page)
- **Video:** Google Meet/Zoom link sent in confirmation email (no in-app video for v1)
- **Deployment:** Vercel
- **Email:** Resend for notifications, booking confirmations, and post-session review requests

### Core Features (v1, 3 weeks)

**Timeline: Week 0 = supply validation (the Assignment). Weeks 1-4 = build. Week 5 = launch.**

1. **Mentor onboarding with manual verification** — mentors sign up via magic link (Resend), fill in their profile including LinkedIn profile URL. Career transition timeline is **self-reported** (structured fields). Founder manually reviews each mentor within 48 hours: checks LinkedIn profile URL against stated career history, marks as verified. "Verified by PathSwitch" badge displayed on approved profiles. Rejected mentors receive a notification email; their data is soft-deleted.
   - **Badge definition:** "Verified by PathSwitch" means the founder has confirmed the mentor's LinkedIn identity and that the stated transition path is consistent with their public LinkedIn profile. It does not constitute endorsement of mentoring quality.
   - **LinkedIn OAuth deferred to v2** — removes API app review dependency (2-4 weeks) and auth complexity. At 5-10 mentors, manual verification is faster and more thorough.

2. **Career transition timeline** — a horizontal row of labeled blocks with arrows (React component), e.g. `Accounting (2018) -> Bootcamp (2020) -> Junior Dev (2021) -> Senior Dev (2024)`. Data source: mentor's self-reported job history entered during onboarding (structured fields, not free text).

3. **Exact-match search** �� filter mentors by:
   - **Transition FROM:** dropdown from curated list (e.g., "Accounting," "Mechanical Engineering," "Biology," "Business Administration")
   - **Transition TO:** dropdown from curated list (e.g., "Software Engineering," "Data Analytics," "Cloud/DevOps," "Product Management")
   - **Country** and **price range** filters
   - **Data model:** During onboarding, mentors pick exactly ONE primary FROM and ONE primary TO value from the curated dropdown lists. These two fields drive search filtering. The timeline component (feature #2) is a separate, richer display showing multiple career steps — it does not drive search.

4. **Mentor profile pages** — verified badge, transition timeline, bio, session price, availability (Cal.com embed), reviews (empty initially, populated after sessions)

5. **Booking + payment** — explicit flow:
   1. Mentee clicks "Book Session" on mentor profile
   2. Mentee selects time slot in Cal.com iframe embedded on mentor profile page
   3. Cal.com confirmation redirects to PathSwitch payment page with Stripe Checkout (not a static Payment Link — use Stripe Checkout Session API for per-booking tracking)
   4. On successful payment, PathSwitch sends confirmation email (Resend) with Google Meet/Zoom link
   5. Session is only considered confirmed once payment succeeds. Cal.com booking confirmation email also fires from Cal.com separately.
   - **Operational note:** In v1, founder monitors Cal.com and Stripe daily to reconcile. Unpaid bookings older than 2 hours are manually cancelled in Cal.com. Stripe Checkout Session metadata includes Cal.com booking ID for tracking.

6. **Post-session review** — Resend sends mentee a review request email 1 hour after scheduled session end time. First question: "Did the session happen?" (yes/no — drives no-show detection). If yes: 1-5 star rating, "Was this mentor relevant to your transition?" (yes/no), "What decision changed after this call?" (free text), "Would you recommend this mentor?" (yes/no). Review emails are only triggered for sessions with confirmed payment status (known v1 limitation: cancelled sessions via Cal.com may still trigger if not synced — founder monitors manually).

7. **Mentee auth + data model** — magic link auth (Resend) required to book. On first auth, a `Mentee` record is created with email. No account required to browse mentor profiles. Intake form deferred to v2 (search filters already capture intent; at 5-10 mentors, founder can email mentees directly for context).

8. **Landing page** — "Find someone who made your exact career switch" with featured transition paths and 3-5 mentor cards

### Cancellation and Disputes

- **Mentee cancellation:** Free cancellation up to 24 hours before session. Within 24 hours, no refund.
- **Mentor no-show:** Detected via mentee self-report (the post-session review email asks "Did the session happen?"). If mentee reports no-show, founder manually verifies and issues a full refund via Stripe. Mentor receives a no-show flag (3 flags = deactivation).
- **Quality disputes:** Mentee can flag a session within 48 hours via reply to review email. Founder manually reviews and issues refund if warranted. No automated dispute resolution in v1.
- **Payout timing:** Platform collects payment at booking. Mentors are paid weekly (every Friday) for completed sessions from the prior week, minus 20% platform fee. Payouts via bank transfer or PayPal (manual in v1).
- **Legal/tax note:** Mentors are treated as independent contractors. Founder collects a simple signed agreement. Consult a local accountant before scaling beyond 10 mentors. Stripe Connect (v2) handles international compliance at scale.

## Reviewer Concerns (acknowledged v1 limitations)

1. **Cal.com + Stripe are not atomically linked.** A mentee could book without paying or pay without a valid booking. Mitigated by founder monitoring daily and cancelling unpaid bookings after 2 hours.

## Design Specifications (from /plan-design-review, 2026-05-21)

### Visual Identity
- **Color palette:** Warm tones (amber/teal or sage/coral). NOT purple/indigo AI defaults. Define CSS variables for the system.
- **Typography:** One intentional typeface (DM Sans or Outfit via Google Fonts). NOT system-ui, Inter, Roboto, or Arial.
- **Signature element:** The career transition timeline IS the brand. Elevated to hero-level prominence on landing page, primary visual on mentor cards, first element on mentor profiles.
- **Anti-patterns (banned):** Purple/indigo gradients, 3-column icon grids, icons-in-colored-circles, centered-everything layout, decorative blobs/waves, emoji as design elements, generic hero copy ("Welcome to PathSwitch"), cookie-cutter section rhythm.
- **Card treatment:** Mentor cards use the transition timeline as the primary visual (not icon-in-circle pattern). Cards are full-width on mobile.

### Navigation
- **Persistent nav (all pages):** Logo (left) | Browse Mentors | How It Works | Sign In / My Account (right)
- **Active page:** Visually highlighted in nav
- **Breadcrumbs:** On mentor profile pages: Home > Mentors > [Mentor Name]
- **Mobile nav:** Hamburger menu. Logo + Browse Mentors + Sign In visible without opening menu. How It Works inside menu.
- **Trunk test:** Cover everything except nav. User should still know: what site (logo), what page (active state + breadcrumb), major sections (Browse, How It Works).

### Landing Page Hierarchy
1. **1st (hero):** Headline that names the pain: "Making a career switch? Talk to someone who already did it." One real transition path as the visual anchor (e.g., "See how Sarah went from Accounting to DevOps in 3 years") -- NOT a stock photo, NOT a generic illustration.
2. **2nd:** CTA + 2-3 featured mentor cards with transition timelines visible. "Find Your Mentor" primary button.
3. **3rd:** Social proof section: "Verified Career Paths" trust signal, session count, aggregate rating.
4. **Page type:** MARKETING/LANDING -- hero reads as one composition, brand-first hierarchy, no dashboard-style card mosaics.

### Mentor Profile Page Hierarchy
1. **1st:** Name + "Verified by PathSwitch" badge + career transition timeline (the proof)
2. **2nd:** Bio + "Why I mentor" section (the personal connection)
3. **3rd:** Price + Cal.com availability embed + "Book Session" CTA (the action)
4. **4th:** Reviews section (the validation). Empty state: "Be the first to share your experience with [Name]" after booking
- **Cal.com embed:** Styled to match PathSwitch using Cal.com embed API theme parameter (primary color, font family, border-radius). Wrapped in "Book a Session" section. Mobile: full-width, min-height 500px.

### Mentor Search/Listing Page
- **Filters:** FROM dropdown + TO dropdown + Country + Price range. On mobile: collapsible filter panel (not 4 dropdowns in a row).
- **Results:** Mentor cards in single column, transition timeline as primary visual on each card.
- **Sort:** Default by relevance (exact FROM+TO match first, then adjacent matches).

### Responsive Specifications
- **Breakpoints:** 375px (phone), 768px (tablet), 1024px+ (desktop)
- **Mobile nav:** Hamburger menu. Logo + Browse + Sign In visible. How It Works inside menu.
- **Career timeline (mobile):** Horizontal scroll, touch-swipeable. NOT wrapped or stacked vertically.
- **Mentor cards (mobile):** Full-width single column. Timeline remains the primary visual element.
- **Cal.com embed (mobile):** Full-width iframe, no side padding, min-height 500px to avoid scroll-in-scroll.
- **Search filters (mobile):** Collapsible filter panel with "Filters" toggle button. NOT 4 dropdowns in a row.
- **Touch targets:** 44px minimum on all interactive elements (buttons, links, form controls).
- **Accessibility:** Keyboard navigation on all interactive elements. ARIA landmarks for main regions. Color contrast ratio minimum 4.5:1 on body text. Focus indicators visible. Form labels always visible (no placeholder-as-label pattern).
